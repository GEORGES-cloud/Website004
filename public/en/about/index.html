About us

Luxor Marbella

Luxor Marbella is a Marbella-based company that manages rentals, renovates and maintains homes for owners who don’t live here all year round.

The key you don’t have to go looking for

We look after homes in and around Marbella for owners who don’t live here all year round. We hold the keys, let you know before we go in, and leave the house as if you were arriving tomorrow.

We work across three lines – rental management, renovations and maintenance – coordinated through a single point of contact. After every visit you receive a report covering what was done, the materials used and what is still outstanding.
