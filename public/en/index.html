Frequently asked questions

What is Luxor Marbella?

Luxor Marbella is a Marbella-based company that manages rentals, renovates and maintains homes for owners who don’t live here all year round.

What services does Luxor Marbella offer?

Luxor Marbella offers three lines of service: full-service property and rental management, renovations and home staging, and home maintenance and cleaning.

Which areas does Luxor Marbella cover?

Luxor Marbella covers Marbella, Puerto Banús, Nueva Andalucía, San Pedro de Alcántara, Benahavís, Estepona, Elviria and Las Chapas, and Sotogrande.

Can I choose just one service?

Yes. You can choose a single service or combine several. If you combine them, we coordinate the visits between ourselves.

Do I need to be in Marbella?

No. We hold the keys, let you know before we go in, and tell you what we’ve done after every visit.

How do I get a quote?

Through the form on the website, on WhatsApp or by calling +34 603 60 55 43.
