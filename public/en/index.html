Frequently asked questions

What is Luxor Marbella?

Luxor Marbella is a property services and project management company based in Marbella: it looks after, maintains and renovates homes for owners who don’t live here all year round, including while they are rented out.

What services does Luxor Marbella offer?

Luxor Marbella offers four lines of service: rental services, project management for renovations and building works, renovations and home staging, and home maintenance and cleaning.

Is Luxor Marbella an estate agent or a letting agency?

No. Luxor Marbella does not list homes on Airbnb, Booking, Idealista or any other portal, does not find guests or tenants and does not set rental prices. It handles the day-to-day running of the rented home (check-ins and check-outs, cleaning, pre-arrival checks and guest support) with the bookings and contracts secured by the owner or their agency.

What are your opening hours and which languages do you speak?

Every day from 9:00 to 20:00, in Spanish and English, by phone and WhatsApp (+34 655 67 36 68) or by email (info@luxormarbella.com).

Which areas does Luxor Marbella cover?

Luxor Marbella covers Marbella, Puerto Banús, Nueva Andalucía, San Pedro de Alcántara, Benahavís, Estepona, Elviria and Las Chapas, and Sotogrande.

Can I choose just one service?

Yes. You can choose a single service or combine several. If you combine them, we coordinate the visits between ourselves.

Do I need to be in Marbella?

No. We hold the keys, let you know before we go in, and tell you what we’ve done after every visit.

How do I get a quote?

Through the form on the website, on WhatsApp or by calling +34 655 67 36 68.
