Renovations and home staging in Marbella

What’s included

Renovations in Marbella, large or small, to make your property more appealing and easier to rent.

  • Full and partial renovations

    From small renovations and repairs to the whole property: bathrooms, kitchens and one-off updates.

  • Rental-ready refurbishment

    We get the property ready before it goes on the rental market, repairing and renewing whatever is needed.

  • Furnishing, décor and home staging

    We furnish and style the property with the listing photos and viewings in mind.

  • Post-renovation cleaning

    When the work is finished, we clean thoroughly and hand the property back ready to move into.

From a small renovation to the whole property

You don’t need a big job to call us. We take on repairs and small renovations just as readily as partial and full renovations.

Designed to rent better

If the property is going to be rented out, the renovation and furnishing are decided with that in mind: it should appeal, stand up to use and be easy to maintain. Afterwards, we can also take care of managing the rental.

Frequently asked questions

Do you do small renovations?

Yes. We do small renovations and repairs, as well as partial and full renovations.

Do you furnish the property?

Yes. We take care of furnishing, décor and home staging.

Who cleans up when the work is finished?

We do. We carry out the post-renovation clean and hand over the property ready to move into.
