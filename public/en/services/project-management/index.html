What’s included

  • Planning and budget

    Before we start, we agree with you what will be done, in what order and how long it will take, and give you a fixed-price quote.

  • Coordinating trades and suppliers

    We schedule every trade and every materials order so the work moves forward without downtime.

  • Site supervision

    We supervise the work in person and send you photo reports on progress and on what is still outstanding, wherever you are.

  • Quality control and handover

    We check every finish before signing it off and hand the property back to you clean and ready to use or rent out.

One person in charge

A project brings together many trades and suppliers. We coordinate them, and you deal with one person who is accountable for the whole project.

For owners who aren’t in Marbella

We keep you up to date with reports and photos, and only ask you about what really needs your decision. The work itself can be carried out by our own renovation team and, once it’s finished, we can also take care of maintenance or rental services.

Frequently asked questions

What does project management for a renovation involve?

It means running the project from start to finish: planning it, budgeting it, coordinating the trades and suppliers, supervising the work and handing it over finished.

Do I need to be in Marbella during the works?

No. We supervise the work for you and send you photo reports on how it’s progressing.

Can I hire you just for project management?

Yes. You can book it on its own or combine it with our renovation, maintenance and rental services.

Is the quote a fixed price?

Yes. Before we start, we agree what will be done, in what order and how long it will take, with a fixed price in writing; any extra is approved before it is done.
