What’s included

  • Maintenance and repairs

    Preventive and corrective, including the pool and garden. We deal with every fault.

  • Cleaning and laundry

    The house clean and the bed linen and towels ready when you arrive.

  • Key holding and inspections

    Key holding and handover. Regular inspections while the house is closed up.

  • Utilities and services

    Utilities, alarm, insurance, phone, internet and domestic help: we arrange it all for you.

  • Rental check-ins and check-outs

    If you rent it out: check-in, check-out, keys, changeover cleaning and support for guests and tenants.

  • Administration

    Payment of the property’s taxes and representation before the owners’ community.

For owners who don’t live here all year round

We let you know before we go in and leave the house as if you were arriving tomorrow.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also look after the pool and garden?

Yes. Maintenance includes the pool and outdoor areas.

Do you hold the keys to the property?

Yes, and we hand them over to whoever you tell us to.

Can you arrange the alarm, insurance or internet?

Yes. We take care of arranging the utilities, alarm, insurance, phone and internet.

Can you get the house ready before I arrive?

Yes. Cleaning, bed linen and towels, and outdoor furniture, all ready the day you arrive.

Do you work with long-term rentals or only holiday lets?

Both: holiday rentals and long-term lets.

Do you work with Airbnb, Booking.com or my agency?

Yes, through any channel. The listing, the price and the bookings are up to you or your agency; we look after the home and whoever is staying there.

Is Luxor Marbella an estate agent or a letting agency?

No. Luxor Marbella does not list homes on Airbnb, Booking or Idealista, does not find guests or tenants and does not set prices. It handles the day-to-day running of the rental (check-ins and check-outs, cleaning and guest support) with the bookings secured by the owner or their agency.
