What’s included

What a house needs when its owner isn’t there: everything working, everything clean, and someone who takes responsibility for it.

  • Maintenance and repairs

    Preventive and reactive maintenance, including the pool and outdoor areas. We check over the property, deal with every fault, and put out and put away the outdoor furniture.

  • Cleaning and laundry

    Home cleaning and laundry, so the house, bed linen and towels are ready when you arrive.

  • Key holding and inspections

    We hold and hand over the keys, and inspect the property regularly while it’s closed up.

  • Utilities and services

    We arrange the utilities, alarm, insurance, phone and internet, and domestic help on your behalf.

  • Administration

    We handle the property’s tax payments and represent you at owners’ community meetings.

For owners who don’t live here all year round

We hold the keys, let you know before we go in, and leave the house as if you were arriving tomorrow.

If you’d also like the property to earn an income while you’re away, we can take care of managing the rental.

A report after every visit

After every visit you receive a report covering what was done, the materials used and what is still outstanding.

If we spot something during a visit that should be repaired or updated, we quote for it before touching anything and carry out the work through our renovation service.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also look after the pool and garden?

Yes. We hold the keys and hand them over to whoever you tell us to.

Can you arrange the alarm, insurance or internet?

Yes. We take care of arranging the utilities, alarm, insurance, phone and internet.
