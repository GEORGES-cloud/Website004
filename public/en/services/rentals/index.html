What’s included

  • Check-ins, check-outs and keys

    We welcome guests and tenants and hand over the keys. When they leave, we collect the keys and check the property.

  • Changeover cleaning and laundry

    We clean the property and handle the laundry between one stay and the next, so everything is ready for every arrival.

  • Ready before every arrival

    Before every arrival we check that everything works and restock what’s needed, so the property is ready.

  • Guest and tenant support

    We’re the point of contact for whoever lives or stays in your home, and for you too. If something goes wrong, our maintenance team sorts it out.

Holiday or long-term

We work with both and adapt the service to each: for holiday rentals, check-ins, check-outs and changeover cleaning; for long-term rentals, tenant support and looking after the property.

Choose only what you need

You can hand us the whole operational side of the rental or just part of it: check-ins and check-outs, cleaning or guest support. Whatever the house needs, we handle with our own maintenance and cleaning service, so you don’t have to coordinate anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with long-term rentals or only holiday lets?

Both. We work with properties let as holiday rentals and on long-term contracts.

Is Luxor Marbella an estate agent or a letting agency?

No. Luxor Marbella does not list homes on Airbnb, Booking, Idealista or any other portal, does not find guests or tenants and does not set rental prices. It handles the day-to-day running of the rented home (check-ins and check-outs, cleaning, pre-arrival checks and guest support) with the bookings and contracts secured by the owner or their agency.

Do you work with Airbnb, Booking.com or my agency?

Yes. We work with the bookings you or your agency bring in, through any channel. The listing, the price and the bookings are up to you or your agency; we take care of the home and whoever is staying in it.

Can I book just the cleaning or the check-ins and check-outs?

Yes. You can hand us the whole operational side of the rental or just the part you need.

Do I need to be in Marbella to rent out my home?

No. We hold the keys and look after the property and whoever is staying in it. You deal with just one person.
