What’s included

We manage the rental of your Marbella home from start to finish, whether holiday or long-term, with a single point of contact for you and for whoever is staying there.

  • Listings on rental platforms

    We prepare the listing and publish it on Airbnb, Idealista and other rental portals.

  • Finding tenants and guests

    Every enquiry is logged in our CRM and followed up until the booking or contract is confirmed.

  • Owner and tenant support

    We’re the point of contact for whoever lives or stays in your home, and for you too. You don’t have to field calls or messages.

  • Rental returns

    We review pricing and occupancy so the property earns what it’s capable of.

Holiday or long-term

We manage both. If you’re not sure which suits you, we’ll work it out with you based on the property, the area and how you want to use it.

Everything that comes with a rental

A rented property needs changeover cleaning, laundry and someone to respond when something goes wrong. We handle it with our own maintenance and cleaning service, so you don’t have to coordinate anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Do you manage long-term rentals or only holiday rentals?

Both. We manage holiday rentals and long-term rentals.

Which portals do you list the property on?

On Airbnb, Idealista and other rental portals.

Do I need to be in Marbella to rent out my home?

No. We hold the keys and look after the property and whoever is staying in it. You deal with just one person.
